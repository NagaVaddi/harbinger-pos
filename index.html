Questions, answered

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The register keeps selling offline and syncs safely when the connection returns. An optional local outlet hub keeps kitchen screens working too.

Is it UAE VAT compliant?

Receipts and invoices carry your TRN and 5% VAT breakdown, with VAT reports for returns. E-invoicing readiness is part of the roadmap.

Can we use our own hardware?

We certify common receipt printers, cash drawers, scanners, scales and card terminals. Ask for the current list.

Do you support Arabic?

Yes — full Arabic and English with right-to-left layouts, bilingual item names and receipts.

Can I connect delivery apps and accounting?

Aggregator, payment and accounting integrations are available through our open API and partner connectors, delivered by phase.
